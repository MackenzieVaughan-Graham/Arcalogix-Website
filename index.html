Making places machine-readable.

Common questions

What does machine-readable mean?

Is this just another digital twin?

A spatial information model is the structured foundation. A digital twin may additionally require ongoing connections to operational data, assets and systems. Those integrations are deployment-specific.

Does Arcalogix replace BIM or facility management software?

No universal replacement is claimed. The intended value is to make spatial information easier to create, reuse and connect with established tools and systems.

Can AI automatically approve building designs?

No. Design and code-related decisions require verified data, appropriate professional judgment and jurisdiction-specific review.
